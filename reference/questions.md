# Questions: ask little, ask well

The user should click, not type. Ask 2-5 questions in total, only ones whose answer changes the design and which you
could not work out from their words, their website or the stored style. Every question: ready options, best guess
first with "(Recommended)", one short line of description each. "Other" is always there for typing.

## How to choose them
1. Cross out what you already know (from the request, the site, the style file).
2. From what is left, pick in this order of value:
   1. **The words** if not given: offer your own draft as the first option ("Use my draft") with the full draft in
      its description, plus 1-2 alternative angles. Never make them type the whole copy.
   2. **The goal or the one action** ("What should people do after seeing it?"), if not obvious.
   3. **Where it will be used** (sets size), if not obvious.
   4. **Their own material**: "Do you have a website/store I can learn your look from?" (new niche, real business),
      "Do you have a photo to use?" (if the look needs a face, product or place), "Logo?".
   5. **Must-haves and must-nots** only when the niche risks it (dietary labels on a menu, a legal line on an ad).
3. **Every question about their own material** (website, photo, logo, prices, dates, names) gets the option
   "I don't have one, make it up". Picking it switches to step 1a of the skill: no more asking for real things.
4. With a stored style: usually just the words and one detail. Never ask about the look again; the mocks did that.
5. Never ask about colours or fonts on a new niche: the mocks answer that by showing, not asking.

## Examples (adapt, don't copy)
- **"menu for my sushi place"**: website? · which dishes and prices (offer: "take them from my website",
  "use placeholders, I'll fill them in", "I'll paste them") · print or screen?
- **"thumbnail for my video about X"**: the video's one promise in 2-4 words (offer 3 drafts) · your face in it?
  (offer: "Yes, I'll give a photo", "No face") · (stored style: skip the rest)
- **"ad for my gym's new year offer"**: the offer exactly (offer drafts if they gave the gist) · where it runs
  (Instagram post / story / poster) · website?
- **"poster for our concert"**: date, place, time if not given (one question, offer "I'll type them" plus
  "use placeholders") · who plays (headliner first) · print or online?
- **"logo for my podcast"**: what the podcast is about in one line (offer 2 drafts) · symbol, monogram or word
  only? (here asking is fine: it changes everything)
- **Something unusual** ("a birthday card for my mum who loves gardening"): who it's for and the occasion are given,
  so ask only: the message inside (offer 3 drafts) · printed or sent on the phone?
